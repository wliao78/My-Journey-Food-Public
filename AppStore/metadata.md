## zh-Hans

Name: 我在旅途-吃喝
Subtitle: 发现附近值得尝试的吃喝
Promotional text: 走到哪，吃到哪。结合位置与出行方式寻找附近值得试的餐饮选择，先看理由、距离与路线，再决定去不去。
Keywords: 美食推荐,附近餐厅,旅行美食,咖啡,步行,自驾,餐饮,地图

Description:
旅行中最难回答的问题之一：这附近吃什么？

我在旅途-吃喝帮你寻找附近值得尝试的餐饮选择。选择步行或开车，浏览推荐卡片，查看地点、距离和路线；不满意时可以刷新，继续寻找。你还可以保存自己的评价，留下真正喜欢的味道。

主要功能：
• 根据当前位置与步行／开车方式探索吃喝选择
• 每轮展示六条推荐，卡片上下浏览
• 查看地址、地图与导航入口
• 保存自己的评价与体验
• 中英文界面随 iPhone 语言设置切换
• 未配置 AI 时可查看明确标注的演示卡片

使用方法：允许定位以查找附近地点；选择出行方式并刷新。若使用 AI 推荐，请在设置中选择 OpenAI、Anthropic Claude 或 Google Gemini，填写自己的 API Key，并同意向该服务商发送生成推荐所需的信息。不同服务商可能产生 API 费用。

请注意：营业时间、菜单、价格、距离和推荐理由可能变化或出现错误，请在出发前向商家核实。演示卡片不是实际营业商家，也不构成真实推荐。定位与第三方 AI 的数据使用方式详见隐私政策。

## en-US

Name: My Journey Food
Subtitle: Find food worth the detour
Promotional text: Explore food and drink nearby, whether you are walking or driving. Browse six suggestions at a time, check the route, and keep the places you enjoyed.
Keywords: food nearby,restaurants,cafes,travel dining,local food,walking,driving,maps

Description:
One of the best travel questions is also the hardest: Where should we eat around here?

My Journey Food helps you explore nearby food and drink. Choose walking or driving, scroll through recommendation cards, and check the location, distance, and route before heading out. Refresh when you want a new set of ideas, and save your own notes about what you tried.

Highlights:
• Nearby food discovery tailored to walking or driving
• Six suggestions per round in a scrollable card feed
• Addresses, map context, and navigation handoff
• Personal notes and ratings
• English and Simplified Chinese interfaces that follow your iPhone language
• Clearly labeled sample cards before AI is configured

Getting started: Allow location access to explore nearby options, choose a travel mode, and refresh. To enable AI recommendations, choose OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini in Settings, enter your own API key, and consent to sharing the information needed for a request. Your provider may charge for API usage.

Important: Opening hours, menus, prices, distances, and AI explanations can change or be inaccurate. Check important details with the venue before visiting. Sample cards are illustrative, not real businesses or verified recommendations. Read the privacy policy for details about location and third-party AI use.
